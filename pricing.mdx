**Upgrading** starts the new plan right away. If your current plan is already paid for, you pay only the price difference for the days left. **Downgrading** takes effect when the current plan's paid period ends, and nothing is refunded.

## How usage is counted

- **Every request counts**, whichever endpoint it calls and whether it succeeds or returns an error.
- **The monthly limit resets on the 1st of each calendar month.** In the month you subscribe, only calls made after the plan starts are counted.
- **A daily limit applies on top of the monthly one.** You can see it and change it in [API Integration](https://app.getswipe.in/user?tab=api_integration). On a bought plan you can raise it to **5,000 calls a day** yourself. For more, contact [support](https://app.getswipe.in/user?tab=api_integration).
- **The [rate limit](/api-conventions#rate-limit) of 1 request per second** applies on every plan.
- **E-invoice generation needs an E-Invoice plan.** A request with `einvoice: true` from any other plan is rejected with `FORBIDDEN`.
- **Once a limit is reached**, requests are rejected with `MONTHLY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` or `DAILY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` until it resets. See [Error codes](/api-reference/error-codes#usage-limit-errors).
