## How usage is counted

- **Every request counts** toward your limits, whichever endpoint it calls and whether it succeeds or returns an error.
- **Monthly limits reset on the 1st** of each calendar month. In the month you subscribe, only calls made after the plan starts are counted.
- **A daily limit applies on top of the monthly one.** You can view and change it in [API Integration](https://app.getswipe.in/user?tab=api_integration); on an API plan you can raise it to 5,000 calls a day yourself.
- **Once a limit is reached**, requests fail with `MONTHLY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` or `DAILY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` until it resets. See [Error codes](/api-reference/error-codes#usage-limit-errors) for how to handle them.
